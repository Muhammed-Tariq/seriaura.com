For progeny and posterity.

Characterising myself has never come easily. I believe that everybody I know has some mental model of myself that approximates, but never matches, who I really am, and I'm no exception to that. A famous William James quote goes, "a man has as many social selves as there are individuals who recognise him and carry an image of him in their mind," and the feeling that this overwhelmingly evokes in me is that of being misunderstood. I hope that this website will make people understand who I am better.

I usually go by the moniker Seriaura (occasionally shortened to Seri) on the internet, but I've also been known as Seriorance, Deceitful, Vyclic, iiSeveranceS, Xxawesomeguy2, and awesomepower21467000, courtesy of some Roblox YouTube channels I made when I was 11. For the time being, I'm indifferent to whether you use my moniker or my real name.

I really like:
  • listening to liquid or dancefloor drum-and-bass — in particular, Liquicity's flavour of it — I struggle to describe why I like this in particular, but it seems to evoke such a broad spectrum of feelings: melancholy, euphoria, emotion, overwhelm, nostalgia — it all took me to solo-tripping Liquicity Festival this year (just three months after I'd turned 18) and it was incredible and lovely;
  • long-distance running — I've ran five half-marathons (mostly local ones: twice Hampton Court, Surrey, Ealing, and the Martian Race) and, by technicality, an ungodly number of 5/10Ks — I DNFed my first marathon, and I'm running the Vitality 10K, the Bath Half, and the Brighton Marathon soon;
  • enjoying the world’s many experiences, basking in the glory;
  • adoring the Josh-isms;
  • not dying, because dying is scary;
  • eating pizza.
