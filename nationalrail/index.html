How to play

Type a station name and press Enter. Each correct answer adds a little more of the railway to your map.

  • Capitalisation, punctuation and accents do not matter. Common abbreviations and a single typo are accepted.
  • If a name matches several stations, enter the region when prompted. Press Escape to cancel.
  • A station shared by several lines counts towards each of them. Repeating an answer will not lose any progress.
  • Drag or zoom the map to explore. Select a line or region in the progress panel to take a closer look.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser. Clearing browser data removes that save.

About this network

The station inventory, service memberships and track geometry are checked against their source data. Coverage is still under review; completion refers to the loaded dataset.
