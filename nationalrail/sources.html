A map you can check.

The game uses a dated, locally stored railway dataset. Your percentage measures this dataset, not a certified total of every UK station.

Network verification is not complete.

All entries in the independent mainline reference lists are represented. Complete heritage coverage, every service's stopping pattern, exact regional boundaries for every small railway, and the predominant operator on each track still need verification. Unassigned stations remain playable; tracks without a reliable service assignment remain grey.

What is included

Passenger boarding locations in the United Kingdom: National Rail, Northern Ireland Railways, London Underground and Overground, the Elizabeth line, DLR, Glasgow Subway, other metros and tramways, plus mapped heritage, miniature and funicular stations. Crown Dependencies are outside this scope. Closed or proposed places are excluded where the source identifies them. Temporary service suspension does not by itself remove an existing station.

Interchanges with the same name at the same place are one guess, credited to every assigned line. Platforms and entrances are not separate guesses. Identically named stations in different places require a choice. The grey “Service assignment unverified” group is an inventory category, not a railway line.

Sources and independent checks

SourceUse and limits
Office of Rail and Road, table 6329Independent reference of 2,595 mainline stations as at 31 March 2026, with CRS codes and regions. Coordinates are transformed from the official British National Grid values. This is not a November inventory; subsequent openings are checked separately.
Translink network map54 Northern Ireland mainline station names, independently reconciled. The map has no explicit publication date; the audit records its retrieval.

TfL Unified APIStation memberships for the 20 TfL rail/tram lines in the snapshot, including the six named Overground lines. Duplicate stop points are reconciled. Separate National Rail operator feeds were investigated but not used to fill gaps because they contain historical memberships and omit recent stations.
OpenStreetMap contributorsMapped UK stations, geographic track vertices and service relations. Every physical track way in the active-rail extraction is retained, including tracks with no matched service. Service relations may be incomplete or stale; they do not establish predominant operator by frequency.
Nottingham Express Transit timetableCorrects David Lane's omission from the OSM stop lists and reconciles Highbury Vale's two branches into one station.
Natural EarthPublic-domain coastline, lake and river geography for the local background fallback. The background is deliberately unlabelled and is not a precision street map.

What has been checked

The build checks independent mainline inventory reconciliation, unique station IDs, valid coordinates, reciprocal station/line memberships, TfL geometry presence and Northern Ireland names. Regression checks cover names, abbreviations, accents, a single substitution/insertion/deletion/transposition, exact CRS codes, ambiguous names, and saved-progress validation.

Source discrepancies are retained in the audit rather than silently discarded. Corrections include Newbury Racecourse's erroneous NaPTAN coordinates, reused heritage CRS placeholders, and the distinct London and Nottingham tram networks. Raw API timestamps do not guarantee every underlying record is current.

Remaining verification

  • See the machine-readable report for the current unresolved records.

A current, licensed national timetable feed is needed to establish all served-station memberships and predominant operators by frequency. Individual heritage and miniature operators also need to be reconciled against their current station lists. Completion messages refer only to the loaded line inventory.
