Clearspace

In order to preserve the integrity of the Primary Logotype, it is important that no other logos, type, or other graphic elements infringe on its space. The minimum clearspace around the logotype is equivalent to the width of a heart.

Smallest size use

The minimum size the Primary Logotype may be used for print and digital applications is determined by the size of your Bard, measured from base to tip, in centimetres or (multiplying by 10) in pixels. If you are unsure, please send evidence to bard@seriaura.com.

Partner logos

Where partner logos are used, we prefer them to be the same height as the logotype’s lettering, excluding the hearts. This is because I do not have a square-dimensioned logo and I have smashed my head against the table for the past four years trying to think of one.
